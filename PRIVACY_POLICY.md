# Privacy Policy for Chiraag e Azaa

**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

**Chiraag e Azaa** ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing an authentic, offline-first Shia recitation and spiritual companion app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you may provide or that may be collected.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Offline-First Architecture
Chiraag e Azaa is designed as an offline-first application. All core content—including recitations, lyrics, Duas, Ziyaraat, Marsias, and Nouhay—is bundled locally in an offline SQLite database on your device. You do not need an account to use the application.

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device
The following information is stored strictly on your local device:
- **Bookmarks & Saved Recitations:** Stored locally in your device's private SQLite database.
- **Reading & Typography Preferences:** Font sizes, line height, paragraph spacing, and reading modes.
- **Digital Tasbih Count:** Your active dhikr count is maintained locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, or sell this information to external servers.

---

## 2. Push Notifications & System Alerts

- **Local Notifications:** The app provides local device reminders for daily recitations and Tasbih. These notifications are scheduled locally by the operating system and do not require your personal data or internet connectivity.
- **Push Notifications (Optional):** If push notifications are enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) generates an anonymous device token solely to deliver announcements and service updates. No personally identifiable information (PII) is linked to this token.

---

## 3. Audio Streaming (Optional)

When streaming audio for recitations, the app connects to secure content delivery networks (CDNs) or media servers to retrieve the requested audio file. Standard network logs (such as IP addresses and requests for specific audio tracks) may be processed by the hosting provider solely for content delivery and streaming performance.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

The app may utilize standard third-party services that collect information used to identify you anonymously:
- **Firebase Messaging / Google Play Services / Apple Push Notification Service (APNs):** For delivering optional notifications.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

Chiraag e Azaa does not address anyone under the age of 13 for personal data collection. The app does not collect personal identification information from children or adults.

---

## 6. Security

We value your trust in using our application. Since we do not collect personal information on remote servers, your reading history, bookmarks, and preferences remain strictly private to your own device.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
